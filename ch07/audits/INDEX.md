Notes for every reader of these sets:

- **The probe sends staff one request in 15.** The gateway probe under both key drills sends chat one request every 2 s and staff, the dense model, one every 30 s (R/commands.txt:2, `--every staff 15 --interval 2 --timeout 400 --max-tokens 8`; R/drills.json:644), as chapter 6's does: on this lab host staff takes tens of seconds a request, and one every 2 s queued its two copies until the users' Gateway cut the waiting requests at its 300 s stream idle timeout (the second attempt, "Superseded sets"). So each pass's staff count is 6 or 7 requests where chat's is 77 to 103.
- **The stream idle timeout answered 504, not 408.** As rendered, the test backend's 330 s silence ended at the users' Gateway's 300 s stream idle timeout with `HTTP/1.1 504 Gateway Timeout`, server `envoy` and the body `stream timeout` (R/pass1/idle-timeout/rendered/headers.txt:1 to :5, body.txt:1), that body being the stream idle timeout's own local reply.
- **request and backendRequest are both 620 s.** The model routes carry the same value in both fields (A/verdict.txt:696, :697), and the API gateway pod's capture shows Envoy passing on `x-envoy-expected-rq-timeout-ms: 620000` (T/captures/2-api-gateway-64bc7b94df-hm4sq-eth0-payload.txt:252, :297). With the two equal the recording cannot tell which field Envoy enforces; no drill lets either fire.
- **The client and the load go through the internal Gateway.** `make client` and `make load` run on the operator's client, whose resolver publishes no platform name, so both pin `serve.ai.tarnwick.example` to the internal Gateway's VIP 10.20.11.200 and say so (N/rec-make-client.txt:5, N/rec-make-load.txt:6); the serving recipe's route is on gw-internal only (N/finish/serving.txt:11).
- **The per-pod probe's times are a guest's clock.** The key probe pod stamps its rounds on its node's clock, which syncs to the lab's resolvers' NTP; each guest's time sync after `make up` and before the down is in N/guest-timesync-after-up.txt and guest-timesync-before-down.txt. Every other stamp is the lab host's.
- **`make load`'s count of requests is redacted.** Quote the failed and cut counts and the PASS lines, each cut inside the line before the marker (N/rec-make-load.txt:7, :9, :10).
