Notes for every reader of these sets:

- **The internet users reach nothing inside in this chapter.** The firewall has the internet side's interface, zone and route and no rule or translation for it, so inet-client's connect to the published address 198.51.100.10 meets `deny-all-log` from `isp` to `isp` and times out after 4 s (A/verdict.txt:1181; T/verdict.txt:20 to :23). Each connect leaves four log lines, the SYN and its three retransmits a second apart (A/split-horizon/traffic-log.txt:6 to :21; T/captures/1-isp-rtr-eth1.txt:2 to :9). The internet population's two requests in the audit are judged unanswered for the same reason (A/verdict.txt:984, :985). Say the firewall refuses the connect, never that the internet Gateway refuses it: no packet of it reaches the bundle (T/verdict.txt:22).
- **The resolvers answered the trace from their caches.** The audit just before the trace had asked each resolver for the same name, so in T and RT each resolver answered the client at once with no query of its own to its authority in the capture window (T/verdict.txt:4, :12, :18; T/TRACE.md:8 to :10). The forwarding itself is the render's (`../bootstrap/ent-dns/dnsmasq.conf`:15, :16; `../bootstrap/dns-res1/named.conf`:38; `../bootstrap/inet-dns-res/named.conf`:38) and the audit's recursion line (A/verdict.txt:1168). Do not print a capture of the forward from these sets.
- **No AAAA query is captured.** The AAAA answers are dig's output, each population asking its own resolver (A/split-horizon/aaaa-*.txt) and the staff client asking ent-dns in the break and the heal (B/probes/staff-break/dig-aaaa.txt, H/probes/staff-heal/dig-aaaa.txt). No set holds a pcap of an AAAA query, so an exhibit titled "captured" quotes dig's printed answer, not a packet capture.
- **The break's stub timings are whole seconds.** The staff client's tools record their start and end as whole seconds (B/probes/staff-break/getaddrinfo.txt:2, `start 1793463539. end 1793463549.`), so "failed in 10.0 s" means 10 s to the second; the client's resolver timeout is 10 s (B/probes/staff-break/resolv.conf:3). curl's "0.0 s" means within the same second.
- **The warm-up connects are in the traffic log.** Before its captures the trace connects once from each population whose connect is answered, from its own source port (T/warmup/enterprise.txt:1, port 42916; T/warmup/nodes.txt:1, port 42917), so the pair's MAC tables hold each lease holder's MAC (spec/known.yaml L1). The staff's warm-up has its own allow line (T/fw/traffic-log-443.txt:10 to :13); quote the trace's own connect at :6 to :9.
