### How to read the drill sets

- **Sessions typed again.** On this host sonic-cli sometimes ended a session early, before its closing `exit`. The drills type such a session again (up to three tries, `common/drill/lib-drill.sh`), and each try is a `RETYPED` line in the drill's `timeline.txt`: 24 pair reads in the keepalive drills, the restore of R2's pass 2 (`restore.txt.try1` echoes `interface Ethernet12` and nothing after), and the fault of R3's `keepalive-and-peer-link` in passes 1 and 3 (`fault.txt.try1` shut Ethernet0 and Ethernet1 and stopped at `interface Ethernet12`; the second try shut Ethernet12: R3/pass1/keepalive-and-peer-link/timeline.txt, the INJECT, RETYPED and INJECTED lines). In those two passes the peer link was down from T0 and the keepalive link from a moment between 4.0 and 12.0 s after it; pass 2 shut all three ports in one session.
- **The keepalive drills' event headings in `DRILLS.md` name the wrong switch.** R2/DRILLS.md:15, :25, :35 and R3/DRILLS.md:15, :21, :27 read "the standby fe-sw1"; fe-sw1 is the active and the drill's target, and the monitor's standby was fe-sw2, as each pass's `analysis.txt` says (R2/pass1/keepalive-link/analysis.txt:5). The summary took the target for the standby. `common/drill/summarize.py` is fixed for later runs, and the recorded files are kept as written: quote the events from `analysis.txt`. Likewise the compare row "every bond on one leg, seconds after the reboot command" (R2/DRILLS.md:65, R3/DRILLS.md:102) is timed from the shut in a keepalive drill (`common/drill/compare_passes.py`, fixed the same way).
- **"Other ids" in a keepalive drill are the ids on the standby's legs.** The tables' "Ping ids crossing fe-sw1" are the ids on the active's legs, which lost nothing; the "Other ids" lost their round trips while the standby's MCLAG PortChannels were down.
